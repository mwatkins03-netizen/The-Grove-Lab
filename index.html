The vision

A place to tinker

The Grove Lab is a tinkering lab. You submit a build idea, and the Academic Innovations Group helps you see if current agentic tools are appropriate or helpful. AIG can use agents to rapidly prototype an app for you and align that with your research or learning goals. The gallery shows what that can look like, sorted by different types of builds.

The build form is what AIG will need from you. It has examples, side-by-side comparisons from the gallery, and sample language to help you describe your idea. It can be used with or without AI-assistance, based on your preference.

Please note, these are prototypes only. They likely should not be deployed in the classroom. Most builds will require some time and may take a week or more based on resources, capacity, and complexity. AIG may also not have the ability to meet all of your requests. Review the guidelines before you submit.

How it works

From idea to prototype

  1. Browse the gallery

    See builds sorted by pathway. Mark the ones close to what you want; they follow you into the form.

  2. Say your idea

    Type it plainly in the "Here's my idea" box. If you choose, optional AI assistance from Jev suggests a build pathway. You can also skip AI and choose yourself.

  3. Fill out the form

    Each pathway has its own questions, an exemplar, and sample language. Skip what doesn't apply.

  4. Download and email

    Download your response and attach it to an email to mwatkins@olemiss.edu.

AI disclosure

This site was coded with Anthropic's Claude under Marc Watkins's direction. Marc supplied the vision and the language about prototypes, timelines, sharing, and the reflection. Accessibility
