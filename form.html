    The idea box sends your text to Jev through a small proxy that holds the API key. You can point it at a different proxy here.

    Clear the form?

    This erases every answer saved in this browser, including your idea, your pathway choices, and any AI suggestions. It can't be undone. Anything you've already downloaded or emailed is not affected.

    The Grove Lab

    A tinkering lab by Marc Watkins. Send build ideas to mwatkins@olemiss.edu.

    Most gallery builds come from Context Is All You Need. Quoted language is Marc's, from Rhetorica.

    AI disclosure

    This site was coded with Anthropic's Claude under Marc Watkins's direction. Marc supplied the vision and the language about prototypes, timelines, sharing, and the reflection. The build form offers optional AI assistance: only if you choose it, Jev, a TypeSafe AI model, reads your idea and suggests a pathway, and its suggestions are labeled as AI-generated. Nothing else on the site uses AI.

    The look of this site is Understory: its colours, marks, and drawings of the Grove are generated in code. The drawings are stylised, not traced from photographs.

    Accessibility

    This site aims for WCAG 2.1 AA: keyboard navigation, visible focus, labeled fields, sufficient contrast, and reduced motion when your system asks for it. If something doesn't work for you, email mwatkins@olemiss.edu.
