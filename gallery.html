AI disclosure

This site was coded with Anthropic's Claude under Marc Watkins's direction. Marc supplied the vision and the language about prototypes, timelines, sharing, and the reflection. Nothing else on the site uses AI.

Accessibility

This site aims for WCAG 2.1 AA: keyboard navigation, visible focus, labeled fields, sufficient contrast, and reduced motion when your system asks for it. If something doesn't work for you, email mwatkins@olemiss.edu.
