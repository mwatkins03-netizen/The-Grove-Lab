AI disclosure

This site was coded with Anthropic's Claude under Marc Watkins's direction. Marc supplied the vision and the language about prototypes, timelines, sharing, and the reflection. The build form offers optional AI assistance: only if you choose it, Jev, a TypeSafe AI model, reads your idea and suggests a pathway, and its suggestions are labeled as AI-generated. Nothing else on the site uses AI.

The look of this site is Understory: its colours, marks, and drawings of the Grove are generated in code. The drawings are stylised, not traced from photographs. The Grove on the home page is painted leaf by leaf, an homage to the painting method of Theora Hamblett (1895–1977) of Oxford, Mississippi; no image of her work is used, and this site is not connected with her estate or the University Museum.

Accessibility

This site aims for WCAG 2.1 AA: keyboard navigation, visible focus, labeled fields, sufficient contrast, and reduced motion when your system asks for it. If something doesn't work for you, email mwatkins@olemiss.edu.
